Baybisoap begins with artisan soap made with natural ingredients. This first collection explores how that same botanical spirit could carry into body care and hair care.

Our visual world is warm, earthy, and a little free-spirited: botanical motifs, sun-washed colors, and room to slow down.

These are proposed product concepts. Names, ingredient ideas, sizes, and final formulas are awaiting confirmation. The illustrations are packaging concepts.
